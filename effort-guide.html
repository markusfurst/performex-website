Effort & Pacing Guide

Most sessions call for an effort, not a fixed pace or power number. Use this to match how each zone should feel to its purpose.

How to use this

RPE is your rating of perceived exertion on a 1 to 10 scale. The talk test is the fastest cross-check: if you can hold a full conversation you are easy, and if you can only manage a word or two you are racing. When a number is missing or feels off on the day, go by feel and keep your easy days genuinely easy.

These zones are guidelines, not absolutes. Heat, fatigue, altitude, and terrain all shift how a given effort feels, so adjust to the conditions rather than forcing a pace.
