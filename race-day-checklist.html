Ironman bag system

In a full or 70.3 Ironman you don't set up at your own rack — you pack colour-coded bags that volunteers hand you. Drop your blue, red and morning-clothes bags at check-in the day before; special-needs bags go in on race morning. These mirror gear already in your pack list, sorted into each official bag. Racing a sprint or Olympic with a self-racked transition? Skip this and use the Transition set-up below instead.

Transition walkthroughs

Rehearse the sequence in your head the night before. On the day, your body just follows the steps.

T1 · Swim → Bike
  1. Run in — remove cap & goggles as you go.
  2. Strip wetsuit fast, towel your feet.
  3. Helmet on and buckled before you touch the bike, then sunglasses.
  4. Socks + bike shoes on.
  5. Grab the bike and run to the mount line.
  6. Mount after the line.
T2 · Bike → Run
  1. Dismount before the line.
  2. Run the bike to your rack spot.
  3. Helmet off first — always before you move your bike.
  4. Swap shoes, apply Vaseline if needed.
  5. Hat / visor on, race belt on.
  6. Grab gels & water if using.
  7. Start running.
One golden rule: helmet on and buckled before you touch your bike in T1, and helmet off after you rack it in T2. Touching your bike with an unbuckled helmet is a penalty in most races.
