How to use these drills

Drills transfer best when you immediately blend them into normal freestyle: do one length of a drill, then swim one length normally. A practical way to run almost any drill set — pick one drill (e.g. Torpedo), do 6–12 × 25 or 4–8 × 50, alternating drill with easy swim, and carry the same technical cue into the easy swim. Stop the moment quality drops.

Each drill links to a short YouTube demonstration. Add a few into your warm-up or main set rather than doing them all at once — quality over quantity.
