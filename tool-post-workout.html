Where to find IF

Open your workout in TrainingPeaks and check the summary. Use the IF from the TSS type that matches your session:

TSS: Bike with a power meter
rTSS: Any run workout (flat or hilly)
hrTSS: Bike without a power meter · Treadmill runs · Hot-weather runs where pace doesn't reflect true effort

Timing

Drink this within 10 minutes of finishing your workout to kickstart glycogen resynthesis. Ideally liquid carbs: a recovery mix, fruit juice, or chocolate milk. Follow with a full meal within 2 hours.

About IF: Intensity Factor reflects how hard your workout was relative to your threshold. For cycling it's derived from power (TSS); for running, from pace and elevation (rTSS). If you train with heart rate only, use the IF from hrTSS, ideal for cycling without a power meter, treadmill runs, or hot-weather running where pace doesn't reflect true effort.

Important: IF is only accurate if your thresholds are set correctly in TrainingPeaks. Keep your threshold pace (run), FTP (bike power), and threshold heart rate up to date. Outdated thresholds will skew your IF and your carb target.

Note: These recommendations are for carbohydrate intake only and do not account for protein or total daily nutrition needs.
