Sweat Rate Lab

Weigh in, train, weigh out. This tells you fluid lost per hour and how much to drink to stay ahead of it.

How to run the test properly

  1. Test three times across the conditions above. Keep effort in Zone 2 and duration similar each time so the results compare cleanly.
  2. Weigh in with no clothes on, twice for accuracy. That's your before weight.
  3. Train ~60 minutes continuous (running is ideal). Strict version: no fluid or food. If you do drink, log the ounces so it's added back in.
  4. Dry off fully with a towel and then weigh yourself, twice again. That's your after weight.
  5. Log the conditions: temperature, humidity, and workout type, so you can see how your rate shifts with heat.

Sweat rate is estimated from body-weight change (1 lb of loss ≈ 0.45 L of fluid), plus any fluid you drank during the session, scaled to an hourly rate. It's a strong starting point for planning hydration, not a medical measurement. Individualise it in training and adjust for longer or harder efforts.
